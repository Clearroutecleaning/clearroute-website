What We Do

Complete Fleet Cleaning
From Top to Bottom

Every service is designed to keep your buses running clean, safe, and ready for Monday morning.

🧹

Interior Vacuuming

Every seat, floor, aisle and corner thoroughly vacuumed before cleaning — removing all dirt and debris from daily routes.

🪣

Deep Interior Clean

Full wipe-down of all interior surfaces including walls, ceiling panels, floors, and seat backs using professional-grade products.

🧴

High-Touch Disinfecting

All high-contact areas sanitized with EPA-approved disinfectants — handrails, door handles, seat backs, overhead bars.

💺

Seat & Upholstery Cleaning

Seats scrubbed to remove stains, grime, and odors. Keeping your passenger area fresh and safe for students every day.

🪟

Window & Glass Cleaning

All interior and exterior glass cleaned streak-free so drivers have full visibility and buses look sharp from the outside.

🚿

Full Exterior Wash

Complete hand wash of the entire bus — body panels, wheel wells, mirrors, lights, and bumpers. Add-on available.

🚌

CDL Bus Repositioning

Our CDL-certified team can move and reposition buses within your yard — your drivers don't need to be present.

📋

Inspection-Ready Detail

We clean with DOT and DOE inspections in mind. Every bus is left in a condition that passes visual inspection standards.
